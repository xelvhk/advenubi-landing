AdveNubi Terms of Use

Effective September 8, 2026

Publisher

Алексей Хведченя, publishing under the AdveNubi brand. Email: advenubi@gmail.com.

Purpose

AdveNubi provides playful foreign-word practice for children aged 4–6 under adult responsibility. It does not replace a teacher, medical assessment, or speech-language evaluation.

Use

The adult controls installation, system permissions, and lesson mode. Recognition indicates only whether the expected word was heard. It does not grade pronunciation, accent, or ability.

The code, illustrations, audio, and brand may not be modified or distributed in violation of their owners' rights.

Availability

Speech recognition and playback depend on the device and system services and may be unavailable. Picture choice remains available as an alternative lesson path.
